# Shocktail.ai — Brand Guide

## Name & Origin

**Shocktail.ai** — a deliberate play on *cocktail*.

- **Shock** = market shocks, factor stress, scenario disruption
- **Tail** = tail risk, fat tails, the part of the distribution that ruins your quarter
- **-tail.ai** = AI-native, domain-clear

**Tagline:** *Market shocks, shaken — not stirred.*

**Secondary lines:**
- "Shake the factors. Serve the insight."
- "Stress tests with a twist."
- "What's your portfolio drinking?"

---

## Voice

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Confident, slightly witty | Brooding finance-bro |
| Plain English for clients | Jargon dumps |
| Precise about math | Hand-wavy AI claims |
| Educational disclaimers | Investment advice tone |
| James Bond nod (sparingly) | Forced puns every sentence |

**Example UI copy:**
- ✅ "Load the 2022 Inflation Shock — shaken with energy and rates."
- ✅ "Your portfolio's drinking a Stagflation Sour today. Expected hangover: −4.2%."
- ❌ "Leverage our synergistic AI-powered risk paradigm."

## Scenario Cocktail Menu (Naming System)

Preset scenarios get memorable names — users remember the brand when they say:

| Internal ID | Cocktail Name | Flavor |
|-------------|---------------|--------|
| 2008 GFC | **The Lehman Last Call** | Everything down, vol through the roof |
| 2020 COVID | **The Lockdown Lime** | Sharp drop, tech recovery twist |
| 2022 inflation | **The Stagflation Sour** | Energy up, growth down |
| Oil shock | **The OPEC Old Fashioned** | Value + energy, airlines bitter |
| Custom | **Build Your Own** | User-mixed |

*Roadmap: let users save and share custom cocktails.*
